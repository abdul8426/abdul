ABOUT

Professional Overview

I'm a Mechanical Design Engineer with over nine years of experience turning complex engineering problems into practical, field-ready solutions — from wind turbine service tooling to automotive plastics welding systems.

As Lead Engineer at HCLTech on the Vestas account, I design tools that keep turbines running: a transformer lid lifting tool that cut downtime 5:1 and saved €3.4M in production costs, and a gearbox O-ring replacement tool that enabled critical on-site repairs. Earlier, at Dukane, I designed welding workstations for major automotive programs — including Ford, Volkswagen and Range Rover suppliers — across vibration, hot gas and hotplate welding technologies. I'm currently pursuing an M.Tech at BITS Pilani while continuing to deliver 350+ engineering solutions a year in my current role.

⛔ THE PROBLEM I'M HERE TO KILL

Unplanned downtime that bleeds money.

A turbine offline for days over a transformer swap. A welding fixture that looked fine on screen but scraps parts on the line. These aren't edge cases — they're the default when design stops at the drawing and never gets tested against the shop floor or the top of a tower. I'm here to close that gap: to design tooling that survives contact with reality, so teams spend less time firefighting and more time building. €3.4M saved and a 5:1 cut in downtime on one tool alone is the kind of result I chase on every project.

⚡ WHY I'M ALL IN

I design for the hands that use it.

Nine years in, the thing that still gets me going isn't the CAD model — it's knowing a technician 80 meters up a turbine, or an operator on an automotive line, is actually relying on something I designed to work, first time, every time. I've moved from vibration-welded automotive tooling at Dukane to critical turbine service tools at HCLTech, and every step has been chasing that same feeling: taking a problem that's costing someone real time or real money, and engineering a solution that simply holds up. I'm pursuing an M.Tech at BITS Pilani because I want to keep getting better at this — not because I have to.
